## 1. Designation of Data Protection Officer (DPO) (UK GDPR Art. 37–39)

Under Article 37(1)(b) of the UK GDPR, designation of a Data Protection Officer is mandatory where the core activities of the controller or processor consist of processing operations that require regular and systematic monitoring of data subjects on a large scale, or where core activities consist of processing on a large scale of special categories of data pursuant to Article 9 (health data).

CantraCare Technologies Ltd. has formally designated an internal Data Protection Officer reporting directly to the Board of Directors.

### 1.1 DPO Contact Details & Registration
- **Appointed DPO**: Eleanor Vance, LLM, CIPP/E, CIPM
- **Contact Email**: `dpo@cantracare.com`
- **Secure Hotline**: +44 (0)20 7946 0199 (Mon–Fri 08:30–17:30 UK Time; Urgent 24/7 Security Escalation)
- **Postal Address**: Office of the DPO, CantraCare Technologies Ltd., 100 Bishopsgate, London, EC2N 4AG, United Kingdom
- **Notification to ICO**: Formally notified to the Information Commissioner's Office via the ICO Data Protection Officer registration portal.

### 1.2 Statutory Tasks of the DPO (Art. 39)
1. **Inform & Advise**: Informing and advising CantraCare, its executive leadership, and development staff of their statutory obligations under UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and CQC fundamental standards.
2. **Monitor Compliance**: Monitoring compliance with policies, data protection impact assessments (DPIAs), staff awareness training, and internal audits.
3. **DPIA Guidance**: Providing expert advice where requested on DPIAs (specifically clinical modules, AI algorithms, and biometric integrations) and monitoring performance under Art. 35.
4. **Cooperate with Supervisory Authority**: Acting as the primary liaison contact point for the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).
5. **Data Subject Point of Contact**: Serving as the confidential contact for data subjects, care home residents, family representatives, and staff regarding their statutory privacy rights.

### 1.3 Professional Independence & Conflict Safeguards (Art. 38)
- The DPO does not hold conflicting executive roles (e.g., Head of Sales, Chief Technology Officer, or Chief Marketing Officer) that determine the commercial purposes and means of processing personal data.
- The DPO reports directly to the Chief Executive Officer and the Board.
- The DPO cannot be dismissed or penalised for performing statutory duties.

## 3. Representative Arrangements (UK GDPR Art. 27 & EU GDPR Art. 27) (A-07)

Under UK GDPR Article 27 and EU GDPR Article 27, cross-border obligations apply where controllers or processors not established in a jurisdiction offer goods or services to, or monitor the behavior of, data subjects in that jurisdiction.

### 3.1 UK Representative Register (For International Tenants / Suppliers)
- **Status of CantraCare**: CantraCare Technologies Ltd. is fully incorporated and headquartered in the United Kingdom (England & Wales). Therefore, CantraCare does not require a third-party UK representative.
- **Non-UK Care Operators**: Non-UK care operators utilizing CantraCare UK services must designate a UK representative under UK GDPR Art. 27 unless an exemption applies.
