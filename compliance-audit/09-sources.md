- **DUAA 2025 commencement:** Part 5 data-protection provisions in force 5 Feb 2026 and s.103 complaints procedure 19 Jun 2026 are taken from two law-firm summaries; the Commencement No. 6 Regulations were not retrieved from legislation.gov.uk in this run. Treat as probable, verify before publishing externally.
- **ICO guidance** on special-category data, DPIAs and transfers is flagged by the ICO as 'under review' for DUAA; the versions cited remain current until replaced.
- **DSPT:** 2025-26 is version 8 (CAF-aligned, deadline 30 Jun 2026). The 2026-27 cycle (v9) was not located; check dsptoolkit.nhs.uk before the next submission.
- **DTAC:** NHS England's DTAC pages are being retired (content frozen 31 Mar 2026, redirecting from April 2026). The successor assessment route must be confirmed.
- **CQC framework:** CQC's single assessment framework is expected to change in late 2026; regulations (SI 2014/2936) are unchanged and remain the binding reference used here.
- **DCB0129/DCB0160:** NHS England states compliance is mandatory under HSCA 2012 s.250 but its page does not address adult-social-care-only suppliers; applicability here is treated as conditional/contractual.
